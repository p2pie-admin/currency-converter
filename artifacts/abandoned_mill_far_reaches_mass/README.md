Former Ramage Paper Mill / Deerfield Glassine paper mill
16 Depot Street, Monroe Bridge, Monroe, MA 01350

Coordinates:
42.72138, -72.93982

Evidence:
- Reddit title places it in the far reaches of Massachusetts.
- Reddit comments point toward the Berkshires / a very small town without its own police.
- Images show a brick paper-mill structure on a rocky river, beside a bridge/railing and a steep wooded slope.
- EPA and FRCOG identify the former Ramage Paper Mill at 16 Depot Street, Monroe, on the Deerfield River, next to Monroe Bridge and a dam.
- OSM/Nominatim resolves 16 Depot Street as Deerfield Glassine paper mill at 42.7213762, -72.9398172.
